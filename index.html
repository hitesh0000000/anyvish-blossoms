How to Care For Your Everlasting Blooms

Simple ways to keep your chenille arrangements vibrant for years

Dusting Your Chenille Blooms expand_more

Once every few months, use a soft makeup brush, clean paintbrush, or a cool hairdryer setting on low speed from 12 inches away to effortlessly dislodge dust particles.

Reshaping Petals & Stems expand_more

Because our blooms feature flexible galvanized craft wire cores, you can gently bend petals more open or closed anytime to suit different vases or arrangements.

Moisture & Sunlight Guidelines expand_more

Never place water in the vase. Chenille wire flourishes best in dry indoor spaces away from direct water splashes to preserve the plush fibers indefinitely.
